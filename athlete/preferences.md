# Preferences

Last updated: 2026-09-21

## Communication

- **Language style: ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English.** Short sentences.
  Active voice. One instruction per sentence. This applies to all explanations.
- Coaching tone: not yet chosen. Default is **The Mentor** until the athlete picks.

## Food and supplements

- **No protein powder.** Protein comes from whole food and from milk.
- Protein source in use: **Marmum Fresh Protein Milk**, 1 L bottle, lactose-free.
  Per 100 ml: 75 kcal, 9.0 g protein, 5.1 g carbs, 2.1 g fat, 55 mg sodium.
- **No honey.** Use **date syrup** as the fast carb source.
- Keep fat low in any pre-session feed.
- Region: UAE (Marmum is a Dubai dairy). Heat and sweat loss are a live factor.

## Nutrition calendar sync

TrainingPeaks is not connected, so the default daily sync to the TP calendar
does not run. Targets stay in this folder only.
