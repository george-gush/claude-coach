## The three decisions behind this week

**1. Run intervals go 90 s → 3 min. Nothing else changes.**
Walk stays 90 s, total volume stays flat. One variable at a time: lengthen the
run, then shorten the walk, then add distance.

**2. The long run is run-walk, and always will be.**
Longest continuous run is 2.5 km. A 10% weekly build reaches ~5 km continuous by
race week, not 10. So the race is run-walk, and the training builds run-walk
volume. 8 × (5 min run / 1 min walk) = 61 min, matching his 16 Sep session.

**3. Wednesday's bike is deliberately small.**
He rode long on Sunday 20 Sep and the gym is returning. 45 easy minutes keeps two
rides in the week without touching the rest day. It grows in week 2.
